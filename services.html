Engineering & automation services

Programming, integration, commissioning and troubleshooting — delivered by people who also build the panels and understand the machine.

What we deliver

Services across the automation lifecycle

From a single programming job to a complete machine modernisation, support is available on-site and in house.

PLC Programming

Logic development, application programming, sequence design and modifications on existing controllers.

HMI Design & Programming

Screen design, alarms, recipes and operator-friendly navigation for machine and plant interfaces.

Servo System Integration

Motion sizing, wiring, parameter setting and tuning for positioning and synchronised axes.

VFD Commissioning

Drive selection support, parameter setup, load trials and energy-efficient operating profiles.

Panel Design & Manufacturing

Schematics, layouts, bills of material, assembly and testing for new and replacement panels.

Machine Modernisation

Upgrading legacy relay logic or obsolete controls to PLC, HMI and drive-based systems.

Installation & Commissioning

On-site electrical installation, system checks, trials and production handover.

Electrical Troubleshooting

Fault finding in control circuits, drives and machine wiring, with corrective recommendations.

Maintenance & Breakdown Support

Preventive checks, breakdown assistance and support to keep production running.

Automation Consultation

Feasibility, control strategy and component selection advice before you invest.

Our approach

Understand the process before the program

Automation problems are rarely solved by software alone. We look at the machine, the operator, the product and the production target before recommending a control solution.

That approach keeps projects practical: fewer unnecessary components, logic that maintenance teams can follow, and documentation that stays useful after commissioning.

  • Machine and process study with your team
  • Clear scope, sequence and deliverables
  • Testing before site commissioning
  • Handover with operator guidance
