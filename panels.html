Electrical control panel manufacturing

From electrical design and component selection to wiring, assembly and testing — panels built for the machine they actually control.

Design to dispatch

Panels engineered around your application

We manufacture electrical control panels for machines, utilities and process plants — including motor control centres, power control centres, PLC and automation panels, drive panels and customised machine control panels.

Each panel starts with the load list and control philosophy, then moves through layout, component selection, wiring, ferruling and testing. Drawings and documentation are prepared so that maintenance teams can work on the panel later without guesswork.

  • Control and power circuit design
  • Panel layout and component selection
  • PLC / HMI and drive integration
  • Wiring, assembly, testing and documentation
