Who we are

Practical engineering for real production floors

Swiftron E-Tech India Pvt. Ltd. is an engineering-focused company engaged in electrical control panel manufacturing, industrial automation products, machinery trading and engineering services.

We provide solutions for industrial customers — from electrical panel design and manufacturing to sourcing automation products, machine sales, system integration, installation and after-sales service.

Our objective is to provide reliable, practical and cost-effective solutions that help industries improve machine performance, operational efficiency, productivity and process reliability.

With our focus on engineering quality, customer requirements and dependable support, Swiftron aims to become a trusted partner for manufacturers, machine builders, OEMs and industrial businesses.
